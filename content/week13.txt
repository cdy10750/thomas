TITLE The Whole Trip
SUBTITLE Full review: arrival to departure
GOAL Put 12 weeks together and walk through a whole visit in Bisaya, from the airport to the goodbye.

C 08 | Thomas | Arriving at the house | Mano, Nay. Mano, Tay. | (Asking for the blessing) Mom, Dad. | From Week 1 - now with both parents.
C 09 | Thomas | Arriving at the house | Naa koy pasalubong para ninyo. | I have gifts for you all. | koy = I have. ninyo = you all.
C 10 | Inday | Arriving at the house | Wow! Salamat, Thomas! | Wow! Thanks, Thomas! | Gifts are often opened later, not in front of you.
C 11 | Nanay | Dinner table | Kaon na ta. Gigutom na siguro ka. | Let's eat. You must be hungry. | siguro = probably, maybe.
C 12 | Thomas | Dinner table | Oo, gigutom kaayo ko. Lami kaayo ni! | Yes, I'm very hungry. This is delicious! | Two Week 2 phrases joined.
C 13 | Dodong | Living room | Kabalo na ka mag-Bisaya? | Can you speak Bisaya now? | The same question as Week 1 - with a different answer.
C 14 | Thomas | Living room | Gamay pa. Pero nagtuon ko matag adlaw. | Still a little. But I study every day. | matag adlaw = every day.
C 15 | Nanay | Living room | Maayo kaayo imong Bisaya! | Your Bisaya is very good! | You've earned this.
C 16 | Thomas | Living room | Salamat! Si Jiana ang akong maestra. | Thanks! Jiana is my teacher. | maestra = teacher (woman).
C 17 | Jiana | Living room | Unsa'y gusto nimong buhaton ugma? | What do you want to do tomorrow? | gusto = want. buhaton = to do.
C 18 | Thomas | Living room | Gusto ko moadto sa dagat. | I want to go to the sea. | Swap the place: sa merkado, sa simbahan, sa Chocolate Hills.
C 19 | Tatay | Outside the house | Kanus-a ka mobalik? | When will you come back? | mobalik = come back.
C 20 | Thomas | Outside the house | Sa sunod tuig, kung itugot sa Ginoo. | Next year, God willing. | kung itugot sa Ginoo = God willing. Very much appreciated in a religious family.
C 21 | Nanay | Airport departure | Text dayon pag-abot nimo. | Text right away when you arrive. | dayon = right away.
C 22 | Thomas | Airport departure | Oo, Nay. Mo-text dayon ko. | Yes, Mom. I'll text right away. | And do it.

CULTURE The welcome party | Don't be surprised if half the family comes to the airport, sometimes with a sign. Greet the elders first, let Kuya take your bag, and be ready for photos.

DAY 4 | Your Bisaya, and plans | Answer the language question with pride, and say what you want to do.
CULTURE Thirteen weeks | Week 1 you answered Kabalo ka mag-Bisaya? with 'just a little'. You still might - but now you can hold a short conversation, ask for repeats, and understand much more than you can say. That's exactly how it should be.
