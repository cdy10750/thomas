TITLE Hot and Tired
SUBTITLE Health, heat, and feeling tired
GOAL Understand when the family checks on you, and tell them how you feel: tired, hot, sleepy, or sick.

DAY 1 | Tired? | After the flight everyone will ask if you're tired. Answer honestly.
CULTURE Being looked after | Expect the family to watch over you closely: if you look tired, hot or quiet, someone will ask. Answering honestly lets them help you, and they want to.

DAY 2 | The heat | Talk about the weather everyone talks about, and accept the fan.
CULTURE Init kaayo! | Bohol is hot and humid all year, with a hotter dry season from March to May. Small talk about the heat is like small talk about the weather at home. Light cotton clothes and lots of water help.

DAY 3 | Rest and sleep | Accept the invitation to rest, and say you're sleepy.
CULTURE Siesta | In the hottest part of the afternoon many people rest or nap. If Nanay says Pahulay sa, it's a kind offer, and taking it is polite.

DAY 4 | Not feeling well | Say where it hurts and ask for medicine.
CULTURE Tambal | Families often have their own remedies: ginger tea (salabat), a balm called Vicks or efficasent oil rubbed on the stomach or forehead. Accept the care - it's a way of showing love.

DAY 5 | Fever, water, and feeling better | Answer the 'are you sick?' questions and tell them you're better.
CULTURE Drink water - the right kind | Drink bottled or filtered water, not tap water. Families usually have a big water dispenser. Asking Pwede ko mangayo og tubig? is always fine.

DAY 6 | Sun, cold, and jet lag | Sunburn at the beach, cold air-conditioning, and jet lag on video calls.
CULTURE Hot outside, freezing inside | Malls, buses and some homes use very strong air-conditioning. Bring a light jacket. Gitugnaw ko is a sentence you may need on a 10-hour bus ride.

MISSION Video-call mission: Week 4
STEP Ask Jiana: Kapoy ka? and Init ba diha? (Is it hot there?)
STEP Tell her how you feel today with one sentence: kapoy, init, gitugnaw, or katulgon.
STEP Pretend you have a headache and say Sakit akong ulo. Ask her what remedy her family would give you.
STEP When she asks Okay ra ka? answer honestly.
STEP Say goodnight with: Katulgon na ko. Maayong gabii!
